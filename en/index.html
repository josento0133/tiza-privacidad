Tiza privacy policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Who is responsible

Tiza is an app for logging gym workouts, published on Google Play by its developer. For any privacy question, write to josejose0133@gmail.com.

What data the app stores

Everything you enter is stored on your phone: your sets and workouts, how you felt each day (if you note it), routines, body weight, profile (height, age, sex, goal, experience and workouts per week), gyms and their notes, settings and, if you set up the AI, your API key. Tiza has no user accounts or servers of its own: the developer doesn't receive and can't see any of this data. Error reports (below) don't include it either.

What data leaves your phone: the AI

The AI is optional. It's only used if you set up an AI with your own API key and use one of its features. Then Tiza sends directly to the AI you chose, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), the data that feature needs:

  • Improve with AI (when you tap it, or on its own if you turn on "Auto Improve with AI"): your profile (age, sex, height, goal and experience), your body weight, your active gym (equipment, available weights and notes), your recent sessions of that exercise, what you've done today, how you feel today (if you noted it) and, if you train with a routine, its goal and that exercise's plan (sets, reps and rest).
  • What to train today recommendation: your workouts from the last few days, the names of your exercises with their muscle group and equipment, your goal, experience, age and sex, and your active gym.
  • AI analysis: how your exercises have changed over the last 60 days (max weight, sets and volume), the summary of your last week and your body weight.
  • Classify with AI: the names of your exercises.

That data doesn't go through any Tiza server. It is received and handled by the AI provider under its own policy:

  • Google (Gemini): policies.google.com/privacy. On the free tier of the Gemini API, Google may use the content to improve its products.
  • Anthropic (Claude): anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • OpenAI (ChatGPT): openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

What data leaves your phone: error reports

If the app fails and "Send error reports" is on (Settings > About; on by default), Tiza sends Sentry, with servers in the European Union, the technical details of the failure: the error and where in the app it happened, the app and Android versions and the phone model. They are used only to find and fix bugs. They don't include your history, your profile, your gyms, your API keys or anything that identifies you, and Tiza also masks any text that looks like an API key before sending it.

Sentry processes this data as a processor under its own policy: sentry.io/privacy.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the rest countdown, alert you when it ends and, if you turn them on, workout reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders: so the end-of-rest alert arrives on time with the screen off. You can deny it; the alert may then arrive a little late.
  • Internet: only to talk to the AI you choose and, if they are on, to send error reports.

Backups

If you turn on automatic backup or tap "Back up now", Tiza saves copies of your data inside the app (the last 10) and the latest one also in your phone's Downloads folder (tiza-backup.db). That copy and the files you export or share don't include your API keys. The Downloads copy is your own file: it isn't deleted when you uninstall the app; delete it yourself if you no longer want it.

Also, if you have Android backup turned on, the system may save the app's data (including your API keys) to your Google account. Tiza has no access to that backup; you manage it from your phone's backup settings.

Security, retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you uninstall the app, which deletes all of it (except the Downloads copy, which is your own file). You can also delete them in the app, in Settings > Data: Delete workouts deletes the logged sets and how you felt each day, and Delete all data also deletes your body weight, profile, routines, gyms, API keys and the backups stored inside the app (your language, theme and other app settings are kept). Your API key can also be deleted on its own in Settings > AI > Delete key. What is sent to the AI is kept by the provider under its policy; to delete it, contact the provider. Connections to the AI use HTTPS and the copies Tiza exports don't include your API keys. To stop sending error reports, turn off Settings > About > Send error reports; to have the ones already sent deleted, write to josejose0133@gmail.com.

Children

Tiza is not intended for children under 18.

Ads

This version of Tiza doesn't show ads. If it includes them in the future, this policy will be updated beforehand with what data they use and how to manage your consent.
