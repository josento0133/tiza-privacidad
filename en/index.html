Tiza privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Who is responsible

Tiza is an app for logging gym workouts, published on Google Play by its developer, Jose Antonio Aguilar Montañez (Josento), who is the controller of the data described in this policy. For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, write to josejose0133@gmail.com.

What data the app stores

Everything you enter is stored on your phone: your sets and workouts, how you felt each day (if you note it), your notes on exercises and workouts, routines, body weight, profile (height, age, sex, goal, experience and workouts per week), gyms and their notes, settings and, if you set up the AI, your API key. Tiza has no user accounts or servers of its own: the developer doesn't receive and can't see any of this data. Error reports (below) don't include it either.

What data leaves your phone: the AI

The AI is optional. It's only used if you set up an AI with your own API key and use one of its features. Then Tiza sends directly to the AI you chose, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), the data that feature needs:

  • Improve with AI (when you tap it, or on its own if you turn on "Auto Improve with AI"): your profile (age, sex, height, goal and experience), your body weight, your active gym (equipment, available weights and notes), your recent sessions of that exercise, what you've done today, how you feel today (if you noted it) and, if you train with a routine, its goal and that exercise's plan (sets, reps and rest).
  • What to train today recommendation: your workouts from the last few days, the names of your exercises with their muscle group and equipment, your goal, experience, age and sex, and your active gym.
  • AI analysis: how your exercises have changed over the last 60 days (max weight, sets and volume), the summary of your last week, your weekly goal and your body weight.
  • Review with AI (when editing a routine): that routine (name, goal, length and exercises with their plan), the muscle group of its exercises, the names of the exercises you already do, your goal, experience, age and sex, and your active gym (equipment and notes).
  • Classify with AI: the names of your exercises.

That data doesn't go through any Tiza server. It is received and handled by the AI provider under its own policy:

  • Google (Gemini): policies.google.com/privacy. On the free tier of the Gemini API, Google may use the content to improve its products.
  • Anthropic (Claude): anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • OpenAI (ChatGPT): openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

What data leaves your phone: error reports

If the app fails and "Send error reports" is on (Settings > About; off by default: they are only sent if you turn it on), Tiza sends Sentry, with servers in the European Union, the technical details of the failure: the error and where in the app it happened, the app and Android versions and the phone model. They are used only to find and fix bugs. They don't include your history, your profile, your gyms, your API keys or your name, and Tiza also masks any text that looks like an API key before sending it. Like any server, Sentry sees your connection's IP address when it receives the report, but it is set up not to store it.

Sentry processes this data as a processor under its own policy: sentry.io/privacy. Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., based in the United States) stores them in the European Union and, if they are accessed from outside it, does so with the safeguards of its data processing agreement (the European Commission's standard contractual clauses). They are deleted automatically after 90 days at most.

What data leaves your phone: Health Connect

If you turn on "Send workouts to Health Connect" (Settings > Data), Tiza writes to Health Connect, Android's on-device store for health data, each workout from the last 30 days that counts, as a strength training session: its start and end time, the exercise type and the list of exercises with their number of sets. It does not send weights, reps, notes or any other data, and it reads nothing from Health Connect. What it writes can be seen by the apps you allow in Health Connect (for example, Google Fit or Samsung Health). Tiza uses this data only so you can see your workouts in those apps, never for advertising or to sell it, and its use complies with the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove the permission or delete what was sent from the Health Connect app.

Why your data is processed (legal basis)

  • Data stored on your phone: it stays on your device and the developer does not receive it.
  • AI: your explicit consent. Some of that data is health data (body weight, how you feel), so it is sent only if you set up the AI, after the app tells you what is sent, and use one of its features (or turn on automatic mode), with your own account with the provider. You can withdraw it at any time by no longer using it or by deleting your API key. The provider processes it under its contract with you and may do so outside the European Economic Area (for example, in the United States) with the safeguards described in its policy.
  • Error reports: your consent, which you give by turning them on in Settings > About. You can withdraw it at any time by turning them off in the same place.
  • Ads: your consent to store or read the advertising ID and for personalized ads; without it, Google may show non-personalized ads using only the data needed to serve them and prevent fraud (Google's legitimate interest).
  • Purchases: giving you what you bought (performance of the contract).
  • Health Connect: your consent, which you give by turning on "Send workouts to Health Connect" and granting it permission. You can withdraw it at any time by turning it off in Tiza or removing the permission in Health Connect.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the rest countdown, alert you when it ends and, if you turn them on, workout reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders: so the end-of-rest alert arrives on time with the screen off. You can deny it; the alert may then arrive a little late.
  • Internet: to talk to the AI you choose, send error reports if they are on, show the ads of the free version and check your purchases with Google Play.
  • Advertising ID: only for the ads of the free version (not used with Tiza Pro).
  • Health Connect (write exercise sessions): only if you turn on "Send workouts to Health Connect", to save your workouts there.

Backups

If you turn on automatic backup or tap "Back up now", Tiza saves copies of your data inside the app (the last 10) and the latest one also in your phone's Downloads folder (tiza-backup.db). That copy and the files you export or share don't include your API keys. The Downloads copy is your own file: it isn't deleted when you uninstall the app; delete it yourself if you no longer want it.

Also, if you have Android backup turned on, the system may save the app's data (including your API keys) to your Google account. Tiza has no access to that backup; you manage it from your phone's backup settings.

Security, retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you uninstall the app, which deletes all of it (except the Downloads copy, which is your own file). You can also delete them in the app, in Settings > Data: Delete workouts deletes the logged sets, how you felt and the notes of each day, and Delete all data also deletes your body weight, profile, exercise notes, routines, gyms, API keys and the backups stored inside the app (your language, theme and other app settings are kept). Your API key can also be deleted on its own in Settings > AI > Delete key. What is sent to the AI is kept by the provider under its policy; to delete it, contact the provider. Connections to the AI use HTTPS and the copies Tiza exports don't include your API keys. To stop sending error reports, turn off Settings > About > Send error reports; to have the ones already sent deleted, write to josejose0133@gmail.com.

What Tiza has already sent to Health Connect is not deleted when you delete your data in Tiza or uninstall it: delete it from the Health Connect app.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, rectify it, erase it, object to its processing, restrict it and take it with you (portability). You control the data on your phone directly: you can see and correct it in the app, export it (Settings > Data, as a backup or as CSV) and delete it as explained above. For error reports, write to josejose0133@gmail.com; for what you have sent to the AI, contact its provider.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or with the data protection authority of your country.

Children

Tiza is not intended for children under 18.

Ads

The free version of Tiza shows a Google AdMob banner (Google Ireland Limited) in History, Records and the set screen. Tiza Pro has no ads. Before loading any ad, the app shows you Google's consent form (in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland).

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, and to personalize them only if you consent, Google receives and processes as an independent controller your phone's advertising ID, your IP address, device and app data and how you interact with ads, also outside the European Economic Area. None of your workout, profile or health data goes to ads. You can change your choice in Settings > About > Ad privacy, and reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Tiza Pro purchases

Tiza Pro is bought through Google Play, which handles the payment under its terms and privacy policy. Tiza doesn't receive your payment details or your name: the app only asks Google Play, from your phone, whether your account has Tiza Pro, and remembers it on the phone to work offline. That information isn't sent to the developer.
